## Mission

Build an independent, genuinely useful Indianapolis-area sewer intelligence and homeowner decision-support resource for residential sewer lateral problems.

The resource helps a homeowner move from "something is wrong with my sewer" to an informed repair decision: what the problem likely is, how it is diagnosed, who is responsible for which part of the line, what repair methods exist, what local rules apply, and who can actually do the work.

Subject scope:

- residential sewer lateral repair
- CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining / sewer lining
- trenchless sewer repair
- pipe bursting
- sewer camera (CCTV) inspection
- clay and cast-iron sewer issues
- root intrusion
- sewer repair beneath driveways and foundations
- related high-ticket residential sewer problems

## Business Model

- **Market:** Indianapolis metro.
- **Initial beachhead:** Lawrence, Indiana.
- **Focus:** residential CIPP / trenchless sewer repair opportunities.
- **Monetization:** the entire lead-generation asset is eventually rented exclusively to one legitimate CIPP/trenchless contractor.
- **Target rent:** approximately $5,000/month (target, not guaranteed; see Economic Objective).
- **Renter qualification:** the renter must be an actual, capable CIPP/trenchless operator serving the covered area. The asset is not rented to a lead reseller or to a contractor who cannot perform trenchless work.

## Future Rental Model

The eventual renter receives:

- exclusive qualified leads/calls
- agreed geographic/service exclusivity
- partner attribution
- lead routing

The renter does NOT automatically receive:

- ownership of the domain
- editorial control

- backlink authority
- unrestricted publishing rights

The independent asset retains its editorial integrity. Partner attribution must be truthful and disclosed (see `04-CONTENT-EDITORIAL-SYSTEM.md` → Disclosure). Rental terms (contract length, pricing, exclusivity boundaries, performance terms) are **NOT YET LOCKED**.

## Customer

**Primary visitor:** a residential homeowner experiencing or investigating a sewer lateral problem.

The homeowner does not necessarily know the term "CIPP", "lateral", or "trenchless". They typically arrive with a symptom (backup, slow drains, roots, odor, a plumber's camera report, a large quote) rather than a method.

Secondary audiences (supporting, not primary): home buyers/sellers and their inspectors and agents, small landlords/property managers.

## Lead Journey

```text
Homeowner notices sewer problem
        ↓
Cleaning / recurring backup / roots
        ↓
Camera diagnosis
        ↓
Structural lateral problem
        ↓
Determine responsibility
        ↓
Compare repair methods
        ↓
CIPP / pipe bursting / excavation / other
        ↓
Qualified repair opportunity
        ↓
Qualified trenchless contractor
```

The site should be useful at every step, including steps where the honest answer is "this does not need trenchless repair" or "this is not your responsibility".

## Strategic Positioning

We are building an **Indianapolis sewer intelligence / resource asset that generates qualified demand.**

We are NOT building:

- an AI SEO content site
- a fake plumbing company
- a mass-produced local SEO site
- a generic rank-and-rent content farm
- a city-page generator
- an AI article factory
- a generic contractor directory

Principle: the site must remain useful and deserving of ranking even if Google could perfectly detect every sentence that received AI assistance. AI may accelerate research, organization, drafting, coding, and analysis; it must not substitute for original evidence, editorial judgment, local research, expert verification, or real utility. Enforcement: `03-GOOGLE-RESILIENCE.md`.

## Economic Objective

Target eventual site rental: **~$5,000/month.**

This is a target, not a guarantee. Rental viability depends on:

- demonstrated volume of *qualified* residential sewer/trenchless opportunities (not raw traffic or raw form fills)
- the renter's close rate, average project value, and margin (contractor economics)
- lead quality, exclusivity, and consistency over time
- verifiable tracking of leads and outcomes

No revenue projection, lead volume, or project-value figure may be presented as fact without first-party evidence.

## Non-Negotiables

These are constitutional principles. The enforceable, detailed rule list lives in `/AGENTS.md` §4; publication gates live in `03-GOOGLE-RESILIENCE.md`.

- **No invented facts:** no invented local facts, municipal policies, permits, pricing, project values, CIPP eligibility, pipe materials, credentials, licenses, statistics, reviews, testimonials, case studies, or first-party data.
- **No fake local presence:** no fake office, address, Google Business Profile, technician, contractor identity, local photos, or reviews.
- **No doorway pages:** no city swaps, no automatic city-page generation, no keyword-variation pages without materially distinct intent and unique value.
- **No mass content:** no bulk AI page generation, no AI-humanizer/detection-evasion workflow.
- **No black-hat SEO:** no PBNs, bought link blasts, hacked links, hidden text, keyword stuffing, fake schema, fake ratings, or scraped content.
- **Editorial independence:** the site is our owned asset; the renter does not control it.
- **Honest disclosure:** the site does not claim to perform plumbing work itself.
